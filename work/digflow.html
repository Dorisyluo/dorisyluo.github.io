How it plays

The digger runs on its own; you only steer. Everything you carve becomes a channel, and a flood pours in behind you along exactly the path you dug. Rocks and fields make you stumble. Wet pockets of dirt burst into springs. Pickups give a short burst of speed.

Stages alternate: first more obstacles, then more water, then more obstacles. There are two maps — farmland and a cave.

The rule that keeps it fair

The flood never outruns a clean, straight dig. Its speed is a fraction of yours. You lose because of what you did — a stumble, a detour, doubling back, opening a spring — never because the treadmill simply got faster than you.

What I changed, and why

WasA course project whose music would expire with the semester.
NowIts tracks carried forward into Flare's first charts.

The platformer's three moves mapped cleanly onto three lanes, so its level beats became the rhythm game's chart structure instead of being thrown away.

How it plays

Achromatic moves between exploration and rhythmic play. In the rhythm sections the level scrolls to the music: each obstacle asks for one of three moves — jump, crouch or dash — and Space triggers the note on the beat. Combo builds score; a miss breaks it. It plays on keyboard or controller.

The soundtrack

The score was written by Berklee College of Music composers and licensed for the project. Two of its tracks later became the first charts for Flare, which is why the two games share a sound.

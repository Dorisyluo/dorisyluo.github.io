How it plays

Each lamp is a flashlight. Every wall its light reaches first dissolves into still points of light, and you can walk through it. A dissolved wall still blocks light, so the beam eats one layer of wall at a time.

Your body blocks light too. Stand in the beam and your shadow keeps the walls behind you shut — so you are always part of the puzzle. Glass lets light through but not you.

What the week was for

Lumen was a week spent learning how to cast light properly. The beam is found the way Nicky Case's 'Sight & Light' does it: rays only go toward the corners of things that block light, and the hit points, sorted by angle, form the exact visibility polygon. That polygon is drawn as the light itself, so what you see is exactly what the raycasts found.

Honest limit: there are only a couple of levels, and the level design is not deep. It is a mechanics study, and it is listed here as one.

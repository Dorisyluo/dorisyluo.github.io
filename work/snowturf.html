The brief

One week, for a Unity course. I set myself one rule: make a game I hadn't played before.

Play it

How it plays

Rolling over fresh snow paints it your colour and makes your ball grow. Big balls paint a wide stripe and hit hard, but they are slow and turn badly. Small balls are quick, but they get knocked flying and drop snow.

Press Space to dump everything you have collected into a snowman. You shrink back to small, and the ground around the snowman stays yours permanently. After 90 seconds, the team with the most ground wins.

Built in a week

It started as Snowball — 3D, single player, roll a ball big enough to reach the Christmas tree — and turned into a four-team turf war in 2D. The scene is generated by an editor script rather than laid out by hand, so the arena, the snow piles and the three opponents can be rebuilt in a click while tuning. The opponents run on a small AI that goes for fresh snow, rams lighter balls and builds snowmen when it gets heavy.
